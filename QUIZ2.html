Reading Passage: “The River of Tomorrow”

In the small mountain village of Lura, water was always a problem. The streams that once flowed through the valley had dried up because of climate change. People had to walk long distances to find clean water, and farming had nearly stopped.

One day, a young engineer named Taro returned from the city. He had studied renewable energy and artificial intelligence. Seeing the struggle of his people, Taro decided to build something that could change their future — an automated water irrigation system powered by solar energy and controlled by smart sensors.

Taro worked for months with the help of local villagers. They carried pipes up the rocky hills and connected them to a hidden mountain spring. Small AI devices monitored the water level and adjusted the flow automatically. When the sunlight was strong, the solar panels charged batteries that kept the system running even at night.

At first, the villagers did not believe it would work. But when the first drops of fresh water reached the village square, everyone cheered. The dry farms turned green again, and children played under the sprinkling fountains. The project didn’t just bring water — it brought hope and pride to the people of Lura.

Years later, engineers from other regions visited Lura to study Taro’s design. His idea spread across the country, helping many other villages survive in a changing world. Taro smiled, knowing that technology and tradition could build a better future together.
